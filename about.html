Who We Are

Our Story, Mission & Values

Xantex was built by traders, for traders — with a single focus on giving every client the best possible trading experience.

Our Story
Founded in 2006 by a team of financial professionals, Xantex set out to democratise access to global financial markets. What began as a small operation has grown into a globally recognised broker trusted by over one million traders across 170+ countries. Our growth has been built on one thing: doing right by our clients.
Our Mission
To provide every trader — from beginner to institutional — with the best possible pricing, technology and client support, without compromise. We believe that access to great trading tools and tight spreads should not be a privilege reserved for large institutions. We are democratising professional trading for everyone.
Our Values
We believe in transparency, integrity and putting clients first. Every decision we make is guided by whether it genuinely benefits the traders we serve. We do not engage in conflicts of interest. We do not mark up prices behind your back. What you see is what you get — always.
Regulation

Regulated & Secure

Xantex is regulated by three of the world's leading financial authorities — providing comprehensive protection for clients globally.

FCA — United Kingdom
Financial Conduct Authority
Licence No. 3082529. The FCA is the UK's top financial regulator — one of the most respected regulatory bodies in the world. FCA regulation means your funds are protected under strict UK financial law, with full compensation scheme eligibility.
CySEC — Cyprus
Cyprus Securities & Exchange Commission
Licence No. 108/334. CySEC regulation grants Xantex full EU passporting rights, allowing us to serve clients across the European Economic Area under a unified regulatory framework. EU clients benefit from full MiFID II protections.
SCB — Bahamas
Securities Commission of the Bahamas
Licence No. SIA-D127. Our SCB licence supports international operations outside the EU and UK, ensuring that clients across the Americas, Asia and beyond are served under a robust and recognised regulatory framework.
