Security Measures

How We Protect You

Multiple layers of security protect your account, your data and your money at every level of our systems.

SSL Encryption
All data transmitted between you and Xantex is encrypted with 256-bit SSL — the same security standard used by the world's major banks.
Two-Factor Authentication
Enable 2FA on your account to add an extra layer of login security — even if your password is ever compromised, your account stays protected.
Segregated Client Funds
Your money is held in segregated accounts at top-tier banks, completely separate from company operating funds — always.
Negative Balance Protection
You can never lose more than your deposit — your balance is automatically protected against adverse market moves, so you cannot go into debt.
Real-Time Fraud Monitoring
Our systems monitor all account activity around the clock and flag suspicious behaviour instantly — before damage can be done.
Instant Account Lock
If we detect unusual access, your account is immediately locked and you are notified — you alone decide when to reopen it.
Best Practices

How to Stay Safe Online

Our systems protect your account from our side — here's how you can protect yourself from yours.

Use a Strong, Unique Password
Use a password that is at least 12 characters long, contains numbers and symbols, and is not used anywhere else.
Enable 2FA Now
Two-factor authentication is one of the single most effective ways to protect your account. Enable it in your account settings today.
Never Share Your Login Details
Xantex staff will never ask for your password. Never share your credentials with anyone, including people claiming to be from support.
Always Log Out on Shared Devices
If you access your account from a shared or public computer, always log out completely when you are done and clear the browser cache.
Check for HTTPS Before Logging In
Always check for the padlock icon and "https://" in your browser's address bar before entering your Xantex login credentials.
